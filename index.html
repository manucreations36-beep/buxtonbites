Terms of Service

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. About these terms

These terms apply when you order from Buxton Bites ("we", "us"), Buxton, Mombasa, Kenya, through this website or app. By ticking the agreement box at checkout and sending your order, you accept them.

2. How ordering works

This site prepares a WhatsApp message with your order. Sending it is a request, not a confirmed order. Your order is confirmed only when Buxton Bites replies on WhatsApp and, for M-Pesa orders, verifies your payment code.

3. Menu, prices and availability

Prices are in Kenya Shillings (KSh). Items may sell out and prices may change; we will tell you on WhatsApp if something in your order is unavailable. Special meals must be ordered in advance.

4. Delivery

Delivery is offered in the listed Mombasa areas. Fees shown are standard estimates; we confirm the final fee on WhatsApp before dispatch. Please give an accurate address and phone number and be reachable when the rider arrives. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.

5. Payment

The site does not take online payments. Pay by M-Pesa Till 1817896 (Buy Goods & Services) or another method agreed on WhatsApp. Enter the correct M-Pesa confirmation code; we check it against our Till before preparing your order. Payments made to a wrong number are not our responsibility.

6. Changes and cancellations

Contact us on WhatsApp as soon as possible to change or cancel. Once food is prepared or dispatched we may be unable to cancel or refund. If there is a problem with your order, tell us promptly so we can make it right.

7. Allergies

Our kitchen handles many ingredients. If you have an allergy or dietary need, tell us in your order notes and on WhatsApp before ordering.

8. Liability

We take care with every order, but to the extent allowed by Kenyan law we are not liable for delays or losses caused by events outside our control. Nothing here limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012.

9. Changes to these terms and contact

We may update these terms; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: WhatsApp 0791 511386 or call 0759 014745.

1. Who we are

Buxton Bites, Buxton, Mombasa, Kenya is the data controller for the information described here. This policy follows Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

2. What we collect

Your name, phone number, delivery address or pickup note, order details, M-Pesa transaction code, order notes, and, if you use it, your location (a GPS position or a map pin you choose). We do not collect your card details and we do not run online payments.

3. How we use it

To prepare, deliver and confirm your order, to verify payment, to contact you about it on WhatsApp or by phone, and to resolve problems. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Your location

Your device's location is requested only to fill in your delivery area and map pin, and only with your permission. You can refuse it and enter your area manually. The pin is included in your WhatsApp order so the rider can find you.

5. Who sees it

Your order is sent through WhatsApp (Meta), so it is also governed by WhatsApp's own privacy terms. Place and address lookups use OpenStreetMap services, which receive the coordinates being looked up. Our riders see only what they need to deliver.

6. Saved on your device

If "Remember my details" is ticked, your name, phone, address and pin are stored only in your browser on this device so checkout is faster next time. We do not receive or store them on a server. Untick the box, use "Not you? Clear", or clear your browser data to remove them.

7. Keeping your data

We keep order messages only as long as needed for service, accounting and disputes, and then delete them.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use, by messaging us on WhatsApp 0791 511386. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.
